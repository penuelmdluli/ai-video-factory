Genesis News — PSL & Mzansi Football

Your daily home for South African football. We follow the big three — Kaizer Chiefs
(Amakhosi), Orlando Pirates (the Buccaneers) and Mamelodi Sundowns (Masandawana) —
across the Betway Premiership, MTN8, Nedbank Cup, Carling Knockout and CAF competitions.

What you get, three times a day:
• Morning — overnight news, team and injury updates
• Midday — transfer talk, press-conference quotes, match previews
• Evening — results, reaction and the debate that matters

We report what real South African football media have actually published, and we credit
them: Soccer Laduma, KickOff, iDiski Times, FARPost, SABC Sport and the official club
channels. Rumours are labelled as rumours. No fake scores, no invented signings.
